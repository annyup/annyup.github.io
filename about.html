Hi, I’m Anny!

I’m a Web Designer + self-taught Graphic Designer based in Ontario, Canada.

I’ve been making things on the internet since I was 9, and somehow that childhood obsession turned into an actual career. Today, I’m a Web Designer at Jonas Software, where I spend my time designing and building digital experiences for the club industry.

I like working where design, code, and a little bit of curiosity overlap. Give me a problem to solve and I’ll probably spend way too much time thinking about how it could look, feel, and work.

Before that, I also had the chance to work in a university environment, which meant designing for all kinds of people and purposes. It taught me how much context matters when you’re designing and that there’s rarely a one-size-fits-all solution.

These days, you can find me...

Learning

Diving deeper into UX/UI and exploring how thoughtful design can make digital experiences feel more intuitive.

Experimenting

Dabbling with AI and figuring out how it can fit into the way I design, create, and work.

And when I’m not designing or coding, you’ll probably find me playing video games, window shopping online, or dreaming about delicious food.

Gotta feed your eyes and brain, right?
