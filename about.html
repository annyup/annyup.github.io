About

Hello, I’m Anny! I’m a Web Designer and self-taught Graphic Designer currently based in Windsor, Ontario. I’m currently a Web Designer at Jonas Software, where I work within the club industry, creating digital experiences that are both functional and visually engaging.

I’ve been coding and designing since the age of 9, and over the years, I’ve turned that early passion into my full-time career. I’ve also had experience working in a university environment, which has given me the opportunity to design for different audiences, needs, and types of digital experiences.

When I’m not designing or coding, you’ll probably find me playing video games, window shopping online, or dreaming about delicious food. Gotta feed your eyes and brain, right?
